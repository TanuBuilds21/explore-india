How we estimate costs

Use this as a planning checklist, then replace estimates with actual prices before booking.

🚆 Train / 🚌 Bus / ✈️ Flight
Enter the return fare for everyone in the transport budget field. Prices depend on route, dates, class and availability.
🚗 Own car / 🏍️ Bike
Estimate round-trip distance ÷ mileage × current fuel price, then add tolls and parking. The website does not know your route distance automatically.
🛏️ Accommodation
Budget defaults: hostel ₹600, budget hotel ₹1,500, standard hotel ₹3,000, premium ₹7,000 per room/night. Edit to match your dates and city.
🍲 Food and daily expenses
Food, local transport and sightseeing are multiplied by the number of people and days. Children are included in headcount for a conservative estimate.
🧾 Before you book
Check official railway/airline/bus sites, hotel cancellation terms, attraction opening hours, permits, weather and safety guidance. This demo does not book or charge you.

Destination details and cost figures are indicative. Always verify transport, accommodation, weather, local rules and attraction timings from official sources before travelling.
